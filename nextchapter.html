What it does

Your reading taste, learned.

Predicts how you'll feel

A personal model trained only on your own ratings tells you whether a book is an Ugh, Good, Great, or Mind-Blown before you start — with a match percentage and the reason why.

Bring your whole shelf

Import your reading history straight from a Goodreads or StoryGraph CSV export — your ratings, shelves, and dates all come along, right on your phone.

The same emoji verdicts

Rate everything on the How To Movie scale you already know — ☹️ Ugh, 😊 Good, 😀 Great, 🤩 Mind Blown.

A For You feed

See the unread books in your library ranked by how much you'll love them, so your next read is always one tap away.

Reading Insights

A personal dashboard of your verdict mix, your best-loved authors, genres and series, your reading by year, and where your taste differs from the crowd.

Covers & details, filled in

Covers, synopses, and reader ratings arrive automatically from Open Library, Google Books, and Apple Books — no data entry.

Private iCloud sync

Your library, ratings, and shelves sync across your own devices through iCloud, so a new phone never means re-importing.

Yours to keep

Everything lives on your device. Export your whole library to a file any time, and dress your shelves in hand-picked book-cloth themes.

The feeling

Less "will I like it?" More "start this one next."

Privacy

Built on your taste. Yours to keep.

Next Chapter is built local-first, with no ads, analytics, or tracking. Your library and ratings live on your device and sync only to your own private iCloud. Predictions are calculated on your device; covers and details come from public book data at Open Library, Google Books, and Apple Books.
