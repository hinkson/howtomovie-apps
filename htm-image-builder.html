What it does

Your movie taste, on your home screen.

Surprise me

One tap and a handful of wallpapers arrive, each a different layout, style and slice of your library. Nothing to choose, nothing to tune, and the whole batch every time.

Eight ways to lay it out

A poster grid, a fanned mosaic, a hero band, a layered collage, a single-film one sheet, a 35mm filmstrip with sprocket holes, a Top 10 podium with number one featured and the ten in the order you choose, and a shelf of spines with the titles running vertically. Top 10 and One Sheet take a heading you write yourself.

Franchises and people

Type John Wick and get the whole run. Type Christopher Nolan and get everything he has directed. Search any franchise or any person on TMDb, or start from a curated shelf. This one needs no account and no library.

Seasonal designs

Midnight Horror runs blood down the screen in October. Snowed In and Lights Up arrive in December, Sweetheart in February, Gold Standard through awards season, and Your Year in Film over the new year. They show up when the season does.

Built from your library

Pull posters from the films you have rated and watchlisted on TMDb, with no hunting for artwork. Filter by rating, genre or studio to get exactly the mix you want.

Light, framed, or matted

Every layout comes in dark, on paper, and framed and matted with a border around each poster. Tune the density, the tilt, and where the band sits.

Every size you need

Your iPhone and iPad at their exact resolution, a 1080p monitor, a 34″ ultrawide, the Studio Display at 5K, the Pro Display XDR at 6016 × 3384, Apple Watch faces, square and vertical social sizes, and print at 300dpi.

A fresh wallpaper every morning

Turn on the morning nudge in Settings, pick a time, and Movie Canvas opens straight into Surprise me with a handful of new wallpapers waiting. It stays off until you turn it on. To skip opening the app, a Shortcuts action pairs with the built-in Set Wallpaper step and puts a new poster wall on your lock screen on a schedule.

Everything you made, kept

Finished wallpapers are saved in the app with the recipe that made them, so you can come back, save one again, or edit it into something new. Save to a Photos album, or share one with a link back.

The feeling

A home screen that's actually yours.

Privacy

Your library stays yours.

Movie Canvas can work from your own TMDb account, or from nothing at all: a franchise or a director wall needs no sign-in. It stores your sign-in and preferences on your device, with no ads, analytics, or tracking. Posters and film data come from TMDb, and generated wallpapers are only ever saved to Photos when you ask.
